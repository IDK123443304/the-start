Classic Burger

A juicy burger with cheese, lettuce, tomato, onion, and sauce.

Calories640 kcal
Prep Time10 min
Cook Time12 min
Servings1 burger

Tools

  • Pan or grill
  • Spatula
  • Knife
  • Cutting board
  • Plate

Ingredients

  • Burger bun
  • Beef patty
  • Cheese slice
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Sauce

How To Cook

Season the patty and cook it on medium heat until browned and juicy.

Toast the burger bun lightly so it stays soft but has a little crunch.

Add lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese, sauce, and the cooked patty between the buns.

Press gently, serve hot, and pair it with fries or a drink.

Varieties

CheeseburgerExtra cheese
Double BurgerTwo patties
Chicken BurgerChicken patty
Veggie BurgerPlant-based
