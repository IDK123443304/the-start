Chicken Wrap

A quick wrap with chicken, veggies, and sauce.

Calories460 kcal
Prep Time10 min
Cook Time10 min
Servings1 wrap

Tools

  • Pan
  • Knife
  • Board
  • Plate

Ingredients

  • Tortilla
  • Chicken
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Mayo or sauce

How To Cook

Season the chicken, then cook it in a pan until golden and fully done.

Warm the tortilla in a pan or microwave so it becomes soft and easy to fold.

Add the chicken, cabbage, tomatoes, lettuce, cheese, and sauce to the tortilla.

Fold the tortilla tightly, toast it lightly if you want, then serve warm.
