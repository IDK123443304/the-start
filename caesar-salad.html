Caesar Salad

Fresh salad with crisp lettuce and creamy dressing.

Calories290 kcal
Prep Time10 min
Cook Time0 min
Servings2 servings

Tools

  • Mixing bowl
  • Tongs
  • Knife
  • Serving bowl

Ingredients

  • Lettuce
  • Croutons
  • Parmesan
  • Caesar dressing
  • Black pepper

How To Cook

Wash and dry the romaine lettuce, then tear it into bite-sized pieces.

Add Caesar dressing and toss lightly to coat the lettuce.

Top with croutons and grated parmesan cheese.

Toss gently, then finish with parmesan shavings and black pepper. Serve immediately.

Varieties

Chicken CaesarMore filling
Shrimp CaesarSeafood twist
Veg CaesarLight version
Extra CrunchMore croutons
